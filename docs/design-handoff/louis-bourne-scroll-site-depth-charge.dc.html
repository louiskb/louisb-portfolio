◆ About Me

About me.

Mixed British-Thai developer based in Thailand, which I now call home after growing up across multiple countries. Fluent in English and Thai, with a long-standing love of tech that led into full-stack development — and an independent trader & financial analyst who reads the markets with a structured, risk-first approach.

I’ve also presented a Thai PBS climate-change documentary series along the Gulf of Thailand.
